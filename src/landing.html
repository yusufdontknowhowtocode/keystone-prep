FBA removals$2 per box
  1. Create the removal order. In Seller Central, enter our address as the ship-to and send us the removal order ID.
  2. We receive and match every box. Removals arrive in waves, often weeks apart. Each box is logged against your ID, so you always know what has landed and what is still missing.
  3. Inspect, count, photograph. A clear count by SKU and condition: sellable, damaged, or customer return. Exactly what you need for any reimbursement claims.
  4. Re-prep, store, or move on. Sellable units can go back into FBA, stay in storage, ship to your own customers, or be set aside for liquidation. Nothing is thrown away without your written approval.

Receiving is $2 per box, waived for boxes with 5+ units. Units we re-prep for FBA are billed at the standard all-in rate. We have consolidated US removals for sellers moving inventory to Amazon Canada, and can relabel for Walmart WFS.

Questions sellers actually ask.

What does the per-unit rate include?

Everything in the standard flow: receiving your inbound shipment, physical inspection and count verification, FNSKU labeling with the manufacturer barcode covered, and outbound box prep to Amazon spec. Add-ons like polybagging or bundling are only billed on SKUs that actually need them.

How fast is turnaround?

Most standard prep ships within 1 to 2 business days once we have your inventory and label files in hand. Receiving is logged the day freight arrives, and you can watch every step in the portal.

Can you receive containers and pallet freight?

Yes. The dock is elevated to trailer height, the lot takes full 53-foot trailers, and our receiving crew has years of international container experience through the parent warehouse operation. LTL, FTL, and parcel all work.

How do I send you inventory?

Get a rate below, and once your account is set up we send the ship-to address and your portal login. You (or your supplier) ship to us, we photograph and log everything at check-in, and you track it live.

Do I have to be in Pennsylvania to use you?

No. Most clients never visit. Suppliers ship directly to our dock from anywhere (domestic parcel, LTL, or international containers), and you watch receiving, prep, and outbound live in the client portal.

Do you handle expiration-dated products?

Yes. Consumables need the expiration date visible on the sellable unit for FBA, and we verify that during prep, including on multipacks and case packs, where the date has to show on the outside of the finished unit.

How does billing work?

You get an itemized invoice after your shipment goes out, payable by card link or bank transfer. No setup fees, no monthly minimums, no long-term contracts.

Can I visit the warehouse?

Please do. Philly-metro sellers are welcome to schedule a walkthrough before sending anything. We would rather you see the racks, the dock, and the prep bench in person than take our word for it.

Send it over and we'll confirm your exact rate by email, usually within a couple of hours. If it's a good fit we'll walk you through the portal.
